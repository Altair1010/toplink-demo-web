- Private or authenticated data.
- Credentials, personal patient records, analytics identities, or administrative endpoints.
- Circumvention of access controls.
- Third-party origins merely linked from the source site.
- Medical-claim amplification or invention beyond source evidence.

## Safety and provenance rules

1. Preserve source URL, capture timestamp, capture method, and status for every route.
2. Separate raw capture, normalized data, inferred design rules, and generated implementation.
