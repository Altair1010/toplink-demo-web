- `FOUNDATION/` preserves the complete v1.0 research/design doctrine **verbatim**.
- `GOVERNANCE/`, `SKILLS/`, `ADAPTERS/`, `PROMPTS/` and operational templates add v2 execution capability.
- Operational summaries MUST NOT replace the foundation chapters.
- Skills should load only the foundation chapters relevant to the active phase to preserve token efficiency.

## No-loss rule

Future revisions may reorganize these chapters, but may not remove or compress a foundation chapter unless:
1. its substantive content is demonstrably preserved elsewhere;
2. a coverage map shows the replacement;
3. the replacement is at least as detailed;
4. source citations/provenance remain attached.
