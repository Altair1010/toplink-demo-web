# Humanizer Art Direction

## Preconditions
- Evidence inventory exists.
- Page narrative hypothesis exists.
- Legacy DESIGN is understood as current default, not mandatory target.

## Produce 3 genuinely different directions
Each must specify:
- thesis;
- evidence anchor;
- emotional register;
- composition grammar;
- typography behavior;
- photography grammar;
- physical→digital material translation;
- motion verbs;
- service-discovery model;
- trust model;
- anti-patterns;
- what legacy design decisions it would supersede.

## External challenger
Anthropic `frontend-design` may be invoked only after an internal evidence-grounded brief exists. Treat its output as a challenger candidate/critique, never as authority.

## Gate
Do not convert a direction into target tokens/code before Human Gate A.
