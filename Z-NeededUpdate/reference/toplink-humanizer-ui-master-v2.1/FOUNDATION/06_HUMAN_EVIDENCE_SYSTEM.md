# Human Evidence System

## Principle

**Do not fabricate humanity. Capture it.**

Real images should clarify or support content, and health guidance recommends realistic images rather than decorative/busy imagery.  
**Nguồn:** [Health Literacy Online — Images](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline/display/section-3-8/)

## Evidence classes

### E1 — People
- founder/owner;
- KTV;
- receptionist;
- staff preparing rooms;
- real interactions.

### E2 — Hands / craft
- hand position;
- preparation;
- towel folding;
- herb handling;
- treatment tools;
- cleaning/reset ritual.

### E3 — Place
- entrance;
- stairs/corridor;
- treatment room;
- quiet corners;
- natural light;
- before opening / after closing.

### E4 — Materials
- wood;
- fabric;
- paper;
- ceramic;
- herbs;
- steam;
- warm light.

### E5 — Micro-stories
- “mỗi sáng làm gì trước khi mở cửa?”;
- “KTV kiểm tra phòng thế nào?”;
- “khách thường hỏi câu gì đầu tiên?”;
- “một chi tiết nhỏ chỉ người làm nghề biết”.

### E6 — Process evidence
- receiving;
- listening;
- preparation;
- care;
- aftercare;
- follow-up.

## Evidence quality rubric

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific | generic | partly specific | uniquely Toplink |
| Real | stock/synthetic | staged | documentary-real |
| Useful | decorative | contextual | explains a step/claim |
| Identifiable | anonymous | some context | person/place/process clear |
| Consent | unknown | partial | documented |
