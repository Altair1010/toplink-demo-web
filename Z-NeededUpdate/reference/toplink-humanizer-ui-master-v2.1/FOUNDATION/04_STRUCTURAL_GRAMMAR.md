## Toplink transformation

### Keep as relationship
- brand before catalogue;
- physical space as trust;
- human team as evidence;
- service discovery after orientation;
- booking is visible throughout.

### Transform
- “Không gian / Dịch vụ / Con người” không nhất thiết hiển thị thành 3 cards;
- cấu trúc có thể thành editorial chapters;
- hierarchy phải dựa vào Toplink user jobs;
- imagery phải lấy từ Toplink.

### Reject
- copy exact styling;
- copy exact wording;
- copy section geometry;
- copy decorative motifs.

## Homepage structural hypothesis

1. **Arrival** — định vị + human cue + action.
2. **What your body may be telling you** — familiar language, not diagnosis.
3. **How Toplink cares** — method/philosophy.
4. **Proof of place** — physical environment.
5. **Proof of hands** — people/process.
6. **Service pathways** — by need, not inventory.
7. **A real visit** — sequence of experience.
8. **Trust/provenance** — boundaries, FAQ, contact.
9. **Booking** — clear next consequence.

Đây là hypothesis cần wireframe/UAT, không phải decision đã lock.
