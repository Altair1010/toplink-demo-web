# Trust / Health / Safety Architecture

## Trust cues supported by research

Clear layout, authority/ownership, ease of use và contact information repeatedly appear as trust/credibility factors in online health information research.  
**Nguồn:** [JMIR 2017 trust review](https://www.jmir.org/2017/6/e218/)

Navigability, aesthetics và ease of understanding also show strong relationships with perceived health-information quality in meta-analysis.  
**Nguồn:** [JMIR 2022 meta-analysis](https://www.jmir.org/2022/4/e36463/)

## Toplink trust layers

### T1 Identity
- legal/brand identity as appropriate;
- physical location;
- contact channel;
- opening hours.

### T2 Human authority
- real person;
- role;
- verified background that may legally be published;
- no inflated title.

### T3 Content provenance
- author/editor;
- updated date;
- sources where factual health claims are made;
- distinction between YHCT concept, inference and modern evidence.

### T4 Scope boundary
- what Toplink provides;
- what it does not provide;
- when another form of care may be appropriate.

### T5 Interaction transparency
- what happens after booking/contact;
- what data is collected;
- who receives it.

### T6 Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 includes requirements around focus visibility/obscuring, target sizing and other interaction accessibility.  
**Nguồn:** [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

## Non-negotiable

Humanizer must never increase medical certainty merely to sound expert.
