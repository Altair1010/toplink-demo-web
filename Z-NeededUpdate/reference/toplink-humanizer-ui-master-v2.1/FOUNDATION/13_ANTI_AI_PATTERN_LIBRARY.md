# Anti-AI Pattern Library

## A. Generic visual probability

Reject by default:
- endless bento;
- 3 equal cards after every heading;
- gradient glow as “premium” shorthand;
- pill-everything;
- oversized centered headline + floating blobs;
- icon-per-sentence;
- random glassmorphism;
- arbitrary marquee;
- identical reveal on every section.

## B. Generic wellness language

Reject:
- cân bằng toàn diện;
- hành trình chữa lành;
- đánh thức năng lượng;
- nâng tầm sức khỏe;
- tận tâm chuyên nghiệp;
- trải nghiệm đẳng cấp;
unless immediately supported by specific evidence.

## C. Fake humanity

Reject:
- AI-written testimonials presented as real;
- invented practitioner quotes;
- fabricated statistics;
- fake “live” activity;
- fake scarcity;
- synthetic portrait presented as staff.

## D. Over-humanization

Also reject:
- deliberate misalignment merely to look handmade;
- illegible typography;
- low contrast “heritage” palette;
- motion that imitates physical ritual but harms usability.

Research does not support trading usability for “human feel”; navigability, aesthetics and comprehensibility are themselves strong quality cues in health information contexts.  
**Nguồn:** [JMIR 2022 meta-analysis](https://www.jmir.org/2022/4/e36463/)
