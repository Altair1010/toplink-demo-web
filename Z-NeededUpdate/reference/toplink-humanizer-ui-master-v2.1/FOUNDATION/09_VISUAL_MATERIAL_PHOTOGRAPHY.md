# Visual / Material / Photography Direction

## Research constraint

Health guidance khuyên hình ảnh hỗ trợ nội dung, realistic và relatable; busy/bright/animated graphics có thể gây distraction hoặc bị hiểu như quảng cáo.  
**Nguồn:** [Health Literacy Online — Images](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline/display/section-3-8/)

## Toplink visual principle

**Visual identity should look sourced from the place, not pasted onto the place.**

## Material translation map

| Physical cue | Digital translation |
|---|---|
| wood | dark grounding surfaces, grain only where subtle |
| paper | warm reading field, matte surface |
| lacquer/red | high-attention event, not universal fill |
| brass/gold | fine rule / seal / provenance accent |
| fabric | softness via crop/light, not fake CSS texture |
| steam | atmospheric photography, not permanent particle effect |

## Photography grammar

### Hero
- real place/person;
- clear depth;
- one focal human/architectural cue;
- leave text-safe negative space deliberately.

### People
- environmental portrait > studio portrait;
- hands and posture visible;
- avoid “team standing in a row” as default.

### Treatment
- explain process;
- avoid ambiguous medical procedure imagery;
- show context.

### Material macro
- herbs, tea, fabric, tools, wood, ceramic;
- use as transitions, not filler.

## Anti-stock test

Reject an image if removing the logo makes it equally plausible for:
- a hotel spa;
- yoga retreat;
- cosmetic clinic;
- generic wellness startup.
