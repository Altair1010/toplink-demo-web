# Linguistic / Knowledge Evidence — Triều Đông Y

## Role

Use Triều Đông Y for domain vocabulary, taxonomy and explanatory grammar — not for medical-claim transplantation.

The supplied package exposes a broad YHCT knowledge architecture and explicitly distinguishes content/evidence categories in its internal mindset/provenance model.  
**Nguồn nội bộ:** `trieu-dong-y-web-dna-package-v1.0.0(1).zip` · SHA-256 `336a446b39c894e2b8fb14234b5e5fe58261305ced88e55a3074773d0428d322` · `UI_MINDSET.md` + content corpus

## Toplink grammar

`đời thường signal → domain term → plain-language bridge → care direction → real process → limits/cautions → next step`

## Vocabulary tiers

- **Tier A:** nặng vai, lạnh chân, khó thả lỏng, ngủ không sâu.
- **Tier B:** khí huyết, kinh lạc, huyệt, làm ấm, dưỡng, thông.
- **Tier C:** specialist language only when necessary and immediately explained.

Health-content guidance prioritizes clear, concise and user-needs-driven language; specialist vocabulary should not replace comprehension.  
**Nguồn:** [NHS health content standard](https://service-manual.nhs.uk/content/standard-for-creating-health-content)  
**Nguồn:** [Health Literacy Online](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline/full/)
