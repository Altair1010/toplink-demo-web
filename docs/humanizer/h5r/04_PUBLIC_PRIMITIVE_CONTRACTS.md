## Shared contract

Every primitive must identify: user question/job; minimum useful response; evidence requirement;
allowed and forbidden states; consequence; DOM/mobile order; keyboard/focus behavior; reduced-motion
equivalent; and genericity failure. Adjacent contracts may share a physical composition. They may
not become eight equal sections or eight mandatory React components.

## Evidence Answer

```text
USER TRUST QUESTION
        |
        v
APPROVED EVIDENCE AVAILABLE?
     /       \
   YES       NO
    |         |
verified      true collapse
asset/fact    no replacement theatre
    \         /
     v       v
CONTINUE TO THE NEXT VALID QUESTION
```

An Evidence Answer may contain a place, public person/role, process, service fact or material only
when its Toplink relationship, provenance, permission/consent and review permit publication. It is
not a gallery, generic trust card, virtue claim or visible provenance wall.

## Composition consequences

- Opening Question and Guided Orientation may share one arrival chapter.
- Clear Boundary follows the answer it qualifies in DOM/mobile order.
- Evidence Answer interrupts only where a trust question is salient.
- Service Scope and Visit/Process Explanation may combine when one reviewed fact answers both.
- Consequence Before Action always visually and semantically precedes its CTA.
- Continue Understanding uses the same visual grammar as care; it is not a second product.
