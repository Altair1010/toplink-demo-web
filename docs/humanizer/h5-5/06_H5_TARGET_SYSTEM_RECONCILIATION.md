## Knowledge contracts proposed by H5.5

These are bounded proposals, not locked contracts, borrowed visual archetypes or production authorization.

```text
FAMILIAR EXPERIENCE OR QUESTION
              │
              ▼
       GUIDED ORIENTATION
              │
       ┌──────┴────────┐
       ▼               ▼
PRACTICAL NEXT     KNOWLEDGE ENTRY
    STEP               │
       │               ▼
       │        KNOWLEDGE DETAIL
       │          ├─ plain term
       │          ├─ limits
       │          ├─ source/review note if verified
       │          └─ related care
       └───────┬───────┘
               ▼
          USER CHOICE
```

Rules:

- no symptom-to-diagnosis mapping;
- no treatment claim or medical authority imported from Triều;
- FAQ is explanatory content, never verification by itself;
- source and reviewer identities must be real, consented and scoped;
- care and knowledge routes link contextually rather than creating an archive catalogue;
- absent source/review evidence remains visibly absent.

## Visual-lane resolution

- Warm crimson derives from approved H3/H4 threshold behavior and current Toplink continuity; exact values remain unapproved.
- Reading surfaces derive from contrast, line length and comprehension; they must not simulate aged paper, folders, stamps or case files.
- Be Vietnam Pro sans-first derives from Vietnamese readability and non-prestige voice.
- Metadata is shown only when a visitor decision needs source, review, time or state context.
- No visual target is authorized by Sen or Triều design tokens.
