## Knowledge-architecture decision

**Verdict: BOUNDED KNOWLEDGE AMENDMENT — PROPOSED.** The current target is too homepage-centric for J-06, service-detail explanation, YHCT terminology and care/knowledge navigation. It does not need Triều's full archive or taxonomy.

Propose four contracts without implementation or target authority:

1. **Knowledge Entry:** a visitor question or familiar experience opens a non-diagnostic learning path.
2. **Knowledge Detail:** term, plain explanation, limits, related care and next choices.
3. **Care ↔ Knowledge Bridge:** contextual reciprocal links between care and explanation.
4. **Source / Review Note:** decision-relevant provenance only; never simulated clinical authority.

They require human approval and Toplink content/job validation. `Related Knowledge` remains a behavior of Knowledge Detail/Quiet Continuation; FAQ is explanatory content, never verification by itself.
