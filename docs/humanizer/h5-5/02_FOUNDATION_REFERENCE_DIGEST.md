### Triều knowledge challenge

```text
VISITOR
  └─ “Tôi đang cần gì?”
       ├─ UNDERSTAND
       │    └─ KNOWLEDGE ENTRY
       │         └─ KNOWLEDGE DETAIL
       │              ├─ RELATED KNOWLEDGE
       │              └─ SOURCE / REVIEW NOTE
       └─ SEEK SUPPORT
            └─ GUIDED INTAKE / SERVICE DETAIL
                 └─ CARE ↔ KNOWLEDGE BRIDGE
                      └─ HUMAN NEXT STEP
```

Findings:

- J-06 is real as a runtime/content problem even though it remains unvalidated as a researched user priority.
- Quiet Continuation is insufficient as the only knowledge contract; it governs low-pressure continuation, not entry/detail/provenance architecture.
- Current `/tin-tuc/[slug]` has title/date/author/related content, but its mock source and generic disclaimer do not establish reviewer, source class or claim status.
- Current service detail mixes scope, suitability, cautions, process, effects and structured data without an authorized review model.
- Toplink does not require Triều's 634-route taxonomy or a “living medical archive.” It requires a small reviewed explanation layer tied to practical questions and service boundaries.

**Knowledge verdict:** `BOUNDED KNOWLEDGE AMENDMENT`.

H5.5 proposes four contracts, not implementation:

1. **Knowledge Entry:** a bounded index of reviewed practical questions/terms; no flat blog dump or full YHCT taxonomy.
2. **Knowledge Detail:** familiar question → plain explanation → Toplink scope/limit → source/review note → related safe next step.
3. **Care ↔ Knowledge Bridge:** bidirectional links between reviewed service/FAQ/orientation context and relevant knowledge; never an automatic fit/diagnosis.
4. **Source / Review Note:** a content-model contract embedded in detail, not a standalone visual badge or archive motif.

Related Knowledge remains a behavior of Knowledge Detail and Quiet Continuation rather than a fifth primitive.
