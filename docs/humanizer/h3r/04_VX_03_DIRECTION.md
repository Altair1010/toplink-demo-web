# 04 — VX-03 Direction

- **ID / status:** `VX-03 — PROPOSED`
- **Evaluation identity:** blind; genealogy intentionally withheld
- **Evidence ceiling:** no approved real-world Toplink people/place/process material

## 1. Visual thesis

**Lời Mở — Lời Chọn:** the visitor's own short phrase opens the experience and returns only at the
decision point, visibly unchanged or edited. Between those two moments, scope, real evidence,
service and visit expectations earn the right to ask for action.

Toplink needs this because it already invites visitors to start from bodily experience, but must
replace automatic interpretation with user control and a truthful, traceable next step.

## 2. Composition

- Five movements: phrase opening; limit; human/place encounter; service plus visit paths; phrase
  return with consequence and learning continuation.
- The opening phrase occupies one bold typographic field; it does not continue as a page-wide line.
- Evidence creates a full-width visual encounter between the two phrase moments.
- Service and visit information branch side by side on wide screens and rejoin only at review.
- The final phrase return is a compact bookend, not an oversized quote or promotional slogan.

## 3. Hierarchy

The visitor's editable words lead twice—at orientation and review. Boundaries interrupt immediately
after the first occurrence. Real evidence leads the middle, then scope and visit expectation. The
final action stays subordinate to the reviewed phrase and its stated consequence.

## 4. Typography behavior

A Vietnamese-safe sans family carries the system. One controlled display-scale sentence creates the
opening signature; all boundaries, evidence, services and states return to a steady reading scale.
Line breaks follow meaning and never crop diacritics. Words entered by the visitor remain plain,
never stylized as a quote or handwriting performance.

## 5. Density

Very low density at the phrase opening, compact and explicit at the boundary, image-led in the
evidence encounter, medium in branched scope/visit paths, and compact again in the final review.
Only one bold-scale event exists.

## 6. Whitespace

Whitespace gives the opening phrase room to be understood, then tightens around limits so they
cannot be skipped. The evidence encounter uses space around context and caption, while branch paths
stay efficiently scannable. Space is never used as lifestyle theatre.

## 7. Image behavior

A verified place/person encounter forms the visual middle and shows context before detail. Images
never carry text chat, anonymous quotes or simulated physiology. When evidence is unavailable, the
middle becomes a concise explanation of what cannot yet be claimed rather than a decorative visual
substitute.

## 8. Human-evidence behavior

The visitor's language is evidence of their current chosen starting point but is never stored or
presented as a testimonial. Toplink people/place/service evidence remains a separate verified
encounter. The two meet only through the visitor's deliberate choice to continue.

## 9. Color behavior

Broad quiet neutrals support reading; one dark grounding field hosts the opening phrase; a warm
action hue marks editability and the final decision. Any additional color must be justified by real
Toplink evidence and state contrast. No social-feed gradient, wellness glow or prestige accent.
Exact values remain open.

## 10. Interaction shape

Visitors choose or edit one sentence starter in a conventional field with obvious edit/remove
controls. The phrase reappears at review only—never as bubbles, message turns or a sticky ticker.
Scope paths are labelled text branches; the no-choice and learning paths stay visible throughout.

## 11. Surface behavior

The opening uses one solid dark surface, the evidence encounter uses an edge-to-edge image or plain
absence state, and all practical chapters use light continuous surfaces. There are no floating
social tiles, glossy layers, decorative threads or perpetual connecting devices.

## 12. Motion expression

- **KHAI:** the complete opening phrase appears in one or two content-level steps, never character
  by character.
- **DẪN:** state focus moves from the phrase to its boundary and chosen branch.
- **TỤ:** the same editable phrase returns at review with consequence.
- **AN:** the final review and all failure states settle without celebration.

Reduced motion shows both bookends and state changes immediately.

## 13. Trust expression

Trust is earned in the middle between what a visitor says and what they are asked to do. A real
place/person/service encounter interrupts the personal phrase before service selection. Health
limits remain visible; missing evidence stays absent; action truth is repeated at final review.

## 14. Mobile translation

The opening phrase wraps naturally in a short field without clipping. Branches become ordered
full-width sections and rejoin at inline review. Evidence preserves context-first image order.
Nothing remains sticky across the page, and DOM order matches reading/focus order.

## 15. Service discovery

Service and visit paths branch only after the evidence encounter. They are organized by reviewed
scope and practical expectation, not by the visitor's initial phrase or assumed condition. `Tôi
chưa biết nên chọn gì` remains a full route to human orientation.

## 16. Knowledge bridge

At the boundary or after a service explanation, `Tôi muốn hiểu thêm` opens a reviewed explanation
within the same light practical surface. It never borrows the opening phrase scale or becomes a
second identity. The visitor may return to care without being matched automatically.

## 17. CTA / consequence expression

The final bookend shows the phrase, edit path, current send state and actual consequence before the
CTA. Failure and retry preserve the phrase safely; uncertainty can return to orientation. A human
handoff is shown only when the recipient/channel and operating path are verified.

## Toplink causal justification

| Choice                                    | Why Toplink needs it                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Phrase appears only at opening and review | preserves user agency without turning the whole site into conversation theatre |
| Evidence sits between the two appearances | forces trust and scope to mediate conversion                                   |
| One bold type event                       | creates recognizability while containing readability risk                      |
| Branches rejoin at consequence            | makes every path end in a truthful user-controlled decision                    |
| Separate learning branch                  | preserves J-06 without visual fragmentation                                    |

## Risks and hard-fail triggers

- Risk: the opening sentence can become fashion typography or a campaign slogan.
- Risk: user-entered health-adjacent language raises privacy and data-minimization concerns.
- Hard fail: quote theatre, social-feed/chat styling, a continuous decorative connector, clipped
  Vietnamese, storing/sending without consent, diagnostic interpretation or evidence substitution.

## H4R tests if selected

Test phrase comprehension and editing with older users; verify that the two appearances are clearly
the same user-controlled content; test privacy copy and no-send state; test the evidence middle with
and without approved imagery; and verify mobile, zoom, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior.
