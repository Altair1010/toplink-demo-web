## Candidate leakage audit

### VX-01

- Sen affects the position of the human/place encounter, not its image ratio, color, typography or
  surface.
- Triều affects only the option to continue into reviewed explanation.
- Review found one residual noun in a service-description sentence. It was removed before final
  scoring; rerun found no system-level collision.
- Residual risk is category drift, not reference derivation.

### VX-02

- The direction begins from zero visual assumptions and uses no historical material metaphor.
- Identity exists through bounded human questions, answer zones, real evidence and consequence.
- Review/reliability information remains content-operational or deliberately disclosed; it is not
  visual decoration.
- This is the cleanest allowed separation of reference structure/content from Toplink appearance.

### VX-03

- The local frontend-design challenger saw only the sanitized brief and proposed one bookend
  treatment.
- Humanizer removed continuous connectors, social/chat grammar, quote theatre and decorative
  physiology.
- The resulting system does not depend on Sen or Triều appearance.
- Residual risk is generic AI wellness/campaign styling, not reference derivation.

- no “living medical archive” or translated equivalent;
- no archive/clinical/administrative material identity;
- no ruled long-form composition or dense visible metadata identity;
- no paper/ink/vermilion authority combination;
- no fixed 680–760px visual signature;

consequence, uncertainty, progressive disclosure and restraint. The appearance is rebuilt.
