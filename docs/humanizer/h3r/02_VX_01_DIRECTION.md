# 02 — VX-01 Direction

- **ID / status:** `VX-01 — PROPOSED`
- **Evaluation identity:** blind; genealogy intentionally withheld
- **Evidence ceiling:** no approved real-world Toplink people/place/process material

## 1. Visual thesis

**Ngưỡng Đón Rõ:** a warm, recognizable arrival opens into calm reading, then real people/place
evidence interrupts the explanation at the exact point trust becomes necessary. Warmth is an entry
and decision cue, not a claim of prestige.

Toplink needs this because its current audience benefits from a familiar, low-disorientation entry,
while the functional sequence requires a visible change from invitation to limits, evidence and
truthful action.

## 2. Composition

- Six movements: arrival; ordinary-language orientation plus boundary; human/place encounter;
  service scope; visit expectation; consequence plus quiet continuation.
- Broad opening and closing fields frame lighter reading chapters.
- Unequal text/image bays replace equal cards; one dominant thought per movement.
- The evidence encounter crosses the normal content alignment so it cannot be mistaken for an
  optional testimonial strip.
- The ready-visitor path remains visible in the arrival movement but receives no greater weight than
  `Tôi chưa biết nên chọn gì`.

## 3. Hierarchy

Large plain-language questions lead; boundaries immediately follow at the next level; supporting
facts and actions sit below. Evidence receives scale through an approved image and a concise public
caption, not through badges. Service names never outrank their scope or limit.

## 4. Typography behavior

Use a Vietnamese-safe sans for body, controls and most headings. A restrained display voice may be
tested only for the two framing moments; it must earn its place through legibility and continuity,
not cultural authority. Short lines, generous leading and visible weight changes support older
readers. No long uppercase Vietnamese text or tiny source labels.

## 5. Density

Medium density at arrival, low density for scope and boundary, high-but-bounded density at service
comparison, then low density before action. This alternation lets visitors pause before choosing
without hiding practical details.

## 6. Whitespace

Whitespace behaves as a change of pace: generous around ordinary-language questions and evidence;
tighter only where service fields must be compared. It never simulates exclusivity or pushes
content below oversized atmosphere.

## 7. Image behavior

When approved evidence exists, show place context before detail and a person within their actual
role before close craft imagery. One large contextual image may interrupt a chapter; secondary
images use varied ratios based on what they explain. Missing material collapses cleanly—no stock,
synthetic person, mood substitute or empty decorative frame.

## 8. Human-evidence behavior

Place, person and service appear as one encounter: where the visitor arrives, who has a public role,
and what scope is available there. Each public caption says only verified facts. Operational source,
permission and review stay in content operations unless a visitor needs them to judge a claim.

## 9. Color behavior

A deep warm anchor marks arrival, active choice and decision review; light neutral fields carry
reading; a dark neutral grounds text and focus. A secondary hue is permitted only if verified
Toplink physical/brand evidence supports it. No metallic prestige cue and no color-only status.
Relationships, not exact values, are proposed here.

## 10. Interaction shape

Controls are broad, direct and familiar. Ordinary-language choices appear as a short, editable list;
service discovery uses a dominant scope path plus supporting rows; consequence expands inline
before any send. Corners and borders remain quiet and practical rather than decorative.

## 11. Surface behavior

Solid matte fields create reliable contrast. A warm field is a bounded event; reading surfaces are
lighter and visually continuous. Depth comes from scale, adjacency and contrast, not shadow stacks,
fake texture or ornamental framing.

## 12. Motion expression

- **KHAI:** one chapter-opening shift at arrival and one at real evidence.
- **DẪN:** a short relation from chosen phrase to visible limit, never a page-long device.
- **TỤ:** selected language gathers into an editable review.
- **AN:** scope, error and action status settle immediately and remain still.

Reduced motion exposes the same final order with no delay.

## 13. Trust expression

Trust appears when real context interrupts explanation before service depth. The absence of evidence
removes or restructures that movement rather than replacing it with virtues, metrics or claims. A
boundary stays adjacent to health-related orientation and a consequence stays adjacent to action.

## 14. Mobile translation

Framing fields become short bands rather than full-screen panels. Image precedes its explanation;
service rows and evidence stack in semantic order. All actions remain inline, touch-friendly and
unobscured. The desktop asymmetry disappears before reading order or focus order does.

## 15. Service discovery

Visitors first meet purpose and evidence, then a scope-led sequence with `Tôi chưa biết nên chọn gì`
at equal status. Scope, limits and verified visit expectation precede duration/price. An already-ready
visitor keeps a direct route without repeated pressure.

## 16. Knowledge bridge

A quiet continuation offers `Tôi muốn hiểu thêm` after a boundary or service explanation. It opens
a reviewed practical question in the same visual system and returns safely to care context; it never
creates a competing knowledge identity or turns the homepage into a catalogue of terms.

## 17. CTA / consequence expression

The main action is a calm decision threshold. Before continuation, it states what information is in
review, whether anything will be sent, the verified recipient/channel if known, and how to edit or
recover. Success language is impossible without real transport confirmation.

## Toplink causal justification

| Choice                                | Why Toplink needs it                                                           |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Warm entry bounded to two moments     | reduces discontinuity for the current audience without using warmth as proof   |
| Evidence interruption before services | prevents a complete-looking interface from substituting for people/place facts |
| Scope-led sequence                    | prevents service prestige and automatic fit inference                          |
| Broad inline consequence              | directly repairs the current false-success risk                                |
| Short learning continuation           | supports J-06 without displacing care orientation                              |

## Risks and hard-fail triggers

- Risk: a warm anchor can regress into generic luxury-health styling.
- Risk: a display voice can imply authority instead of helping reading.
- Hard fail: prestige colors/ornament, stock mood imagery, repeated early CTA, fabricated evidence,
  unclear consequence, diagnostic output or inaccessible contrast/type.

## H4R tests if selected

Test whether the framing event remains warm but non-luxury; whether evidence interruption feels
human rather than promotional; whether service rows remain scannable at mobile/200% zoom; and
whether the direct route stays visible without conversion pressure.
