## Decision

- **Decision ID:** `DEC-H6F-PRODUCT-SCOPE-01`
- **Baseline:** `2153f1721ff7c27101151489b4e56e3d84211580`

- **Product role:** information, understanding and exploration before an optional human handoff.

The Toplink website is not a booking application. It does not reserve time, collect appointment
requests, choose a service for a visitor, manage booking state, send booking data or confirm a
booking.

## Authority boundaries

- The human decision verifies the channel types `Zalo` and `Facebook Page` only.
- No verified channel URL, account, owner, response time, address, phone or opening hours was found
  in the approved operational sources at this gate.
- Both destination URLs therefore remain `CONTACT_URL_DEFERRED` and must not be guessed.
- M3P remains `DEFERRED TO ADMIN`.
- M4 becomes `DEPRECATED — REMOVED FROM TARGET PRODUCT`.
- Historical Humanizer artifacts remain provenance and are not rewritten to erase the old booking
  path.

## Release consequence

The release surface must contain no booking route, booking UI, booking state, booking query path or
booking transport. Unsupported commercial, evidence-dependent and maintainer/specimen routes must
not be generated by the static production export.
