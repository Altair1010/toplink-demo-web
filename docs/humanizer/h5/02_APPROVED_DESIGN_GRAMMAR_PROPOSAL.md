# 02 — Approved Design Grammar Proposal

**Status:** `PROPOSED FOR GATE B`

**Decision ID:** `DEC-H5-GB-01`

**Name:** Hồ Sơ Sống — bounded system grammar

“Approved” in this template title refers to the package artifact type. This instance is not approved or locked until the human Gate B decision.

## Hierarchy

- Meaning precedes metadata: question/answer → scope or evidence → consequence → optional provenance detail.
- Each chapter has one dominant thought and answers a named visitor job.
- Trust is demonstrated before deeper service exploration; action consequence is visible before send.
- Public metadata is the minimum needed for comprehension and accountability. Operational provenance stays in the content system unless it materially helps the visitor.
- Internal ED labels, review mechanics and development annotations never become public authority badges.

## Density

- Narrative rhythm: `bounded entry → readable intake → explicit boundary → evidence interruption → indexed scope → quiet process → grounded action → low-pressure continuation`.
- Alternate dense records with reading space and documentary pauses; do not render eight equal sections.
- Compact records are reserved for facts with a real owner/scope/state. Missing data does not justify a decorative row.
- Mobile collapses metadata before primary meaning and never turns the page into an endless stack of equal cards.

## Composition

- Paper/ink is the dominant reading field. A warm threshold occurs only at opening and decision/consequence.
- Unequal columns are allowed when they express evidence/context relationships; DOM order remains the reading order.
- Rules and whitespace separate fact from interpretation, claim from limit and request from confirmation.
- Proof context precedes detail. Service scope is an editorial register, not a marketplace grid. Visit/process is a truthful sequence, not ritual prose.
- The editable phrase rail/seam is local to guided orientation and ends before the action receipt.

## Typography

- **Family recommendation:** keep the existing Vietnamese-safe `Be Vietnam Pro` as the sole target family for hero, chapter, body and UI roles. No new font or dependency is proposed.
- **Weights:** 400 for body, 500 for emphasis/metadata when needed, 600 for headings and actions. Weight and scale create hierarchy; prestige serif does not.
- **Display/hero:** short, sentence-case Vietnamese; strong line breaks; never crop diacritics; target roughly 2–4 lines, not a full-screen slogan by default.
- **Chapter/heading:** direct question or accountable statement; no uppercase Vietnamese headings.
- **Body:** production baseline at least 18px, generous line height around 1.55–1.7 and preferred measure 55–72 characters.
- **Utility/metadata:** sentence case, public text normally at least 16px; uppercase only for very short non-essential labels with restrained tracking.
- **Action:** 600 weight, clear verb + object/consequence; no urgency theatre.
- At 375px and 200% zoom, scale responds to content and available measure; no fixed line count, clipped accents or horizontal reading.

## Color/material semantics

- Paper/ink dominates. Contrast, not ornament, carries hierarchy.
- Warm crimson is the candidate threshold anchor for HP-01/HP-07, selected/action states and focus-compatible emphasis; its exact hex is not locked by H5 and must be contrast-tested at migration.
- Gold is not a target premium code. Jade, wood, lacquer, herb, fabric or grain do not enter the target merely because Runtime Truth or a reference contains them.
- Boundary, warning and state never rely on color alone.
- Digital material may translate real Toplink physical evidence after ED-03/08 exists. Until then lock behavior only: matte reading fields, restrained rules, documentary crop and rare attention fields.
- No fake paper grain, wood, lacquer, stamp, torn edge, herb decoration or archive cosplay.

## Photography

- Real Toplink evidence only, with consent/provenance and a content job.
- Sequence context before detail: place wide → person/process medium → craft/material detail where appropriate.
- Environmental portrait beats studio/team row; hands require owner/action/context and cannot be anonymous wellness shorthand.
- Crop preserves the evidence needed to understand the person, place or action. Captions state the minimum public who/role/place/time context permitted.
- When evidence is missing, omit/restructure; do not use stock, synthetic staff, other-brand assets, abstract wellness illustration or H4 placeholders.

## Component grammar

The reusable editorial primitives are:

1. Cover Record
2. Guided Intake
3. Scope Boundary
4. Proof Index
5. Service Scope Register
6. Visit / Process Record
7. Action Receipt
8. Quiet Continuation

Every primitive must exist because of a visitor job, evidence, state, narrative role or consequence. Component appearance is subordinate to its contract in artifact 04.

## Content voice

`Toplink = professional specificity + plain Vietnamese + calm responsibility + clear limits + visible consequence.`

- Speak from an accountable person/system; prefer observation and scope over virtue claims.
- Explain any necessary domain term immediately in familiar language.
- State uncertainty before inviting action and make the no-choice route first-class.
- Never inflate medical certainty, invent evidence or substitute slogans for people/process facts.

## Motion verbs

- **KHAI:** open a major chapter or threshold once.
- **DẪN:** clarify a real relationship or progress path.
- **TỤ:** converge user-provided context into an editable review.
- **AN:** settle a state or consequence without celebration.
- Advanced choreography remains GSAP-only; simple micro-state changes may use CSS. Reduced motion shows the same final information immediately.

## Responsive rules

- Validate 375, 768, 1280, 1440 and 200% zoom; these are acceptance widths, not permission to overfit exact breakpoints.
- Narrative DOM order equals reading/focus order. Desktop asymmetry must collapse without reordering meaning.
- Metadata and decorative context collapse before primary meaning; public tables never require horizontal scrolling.
- Proof context precedes detail; evidence sequences preserve wide/context before detail.
- Warm threshold becomes a shorter band on mobile. The guided phrase remains editable and the action consequence stays inline.
- Practical interactive target remains at least the repo baseline of 44px; sticky actions cannot obscure content/focus.

## Accessibility constraints

- Visible `:focus-visible`, keyboard operation, semantic headings, labels/instructions and state announcements where appropriate.
- No color-only meaning; sufficient contrast for every text/state pairing.
- Vietnamese diacritics remain fully visible at all responsive states and 200% zoom.
- Reduced-motion parity is mandatory; no essential information waits for animation.
- Errors identify the problem, move/announce focus deliberately and preserve only safe data.
- No primitive may be approved if it requires weakening these constraints.

## Anti-patterns

Reject bento as generic grammar; repeated heading/paragraph/three-card sections; glass, glow and decorative gradient; pill-everything; generic luxury serif; gold-premium shorthand; abstract wellness imagery; anonymous hands; fake review/metric/live status/medical authority; ornamental metadata; archive cosplay; and evidence-free “human imperfection”. Also reject the slogans “tận tâm chuyên nghiệp”, “đẳng cấp”, “chữa lành”, “cân bằng toàn diện” and pseudo-clinical certainty unless a specific, permitted fact genuinely warrants different wording.
