## Invariant check

- No framework, rendering, deployment, motion-engine, scroll-engine, backend/provider or data architecture change is proposed by H5.
- Booking transport is a future engineering decision requiring ED-06, security/privacy review and explicit approval; it is not silently bundled into Gate B.
- Semantic alias migration must be staged; no mass search/replace or raw-token deletion before consumer count is zero.
- `tokens.css`, `skins.css` and `DESIGN.md` remain Runtime Truth until an approved migration is implemented, verified and released.

## Migration sequence proposal

1. Human approves exact Gate B system or requests revisions.
2. Build a fresh consumer inventory and exact current→target diff.
3. Migration Gate approves named batches, files, evidence dependencies, rollback and architecture/security decisions separately.
4. Introduce semantic aliases and foundational type/color rules without deleting compatibility tokens.
5. Migrate editorial primitives in narrative order with state/content tests; booking transport remains its own high-risk batch.
6. Verify repo, browser, responsive, state, accessibility, evidence and Humanizer rubric.
7. Re-baseline `DESIGN.md` and Runtime Truth only after successful release.

## Rollback policy

Each migration batch must be independently revertible. Keep legacy aliases/components until its replacement passes and no authorized consumer depends on the old contract. For evidence failures, rollback means omit/restructure — never repopulate with stock, mock facts or H4 placeholders. For booking failure, fail closed to review/contact-only behavior and never display `CONFIRMED` without end-to-end proof.
