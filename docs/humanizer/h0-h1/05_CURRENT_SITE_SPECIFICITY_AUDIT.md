**Test:** if the Toplink logo is removed, could this section belong to another spa, wellness, or Đông-y brand?
**Scope:** audit only; classifications do not authorize redesign.

## C/D surfaces retained as audit targets

1. Homepage hero.
2. “Một ngày ở Y Viện”.
3. “Không gian như liệu pháp” and all space pages that currently imply unknown/other-brand images are Toplink.
4. About/value/team/review surfaces.
5. Contact/location and booking success/consequence wording.
6. Final CTA.

These are not instructions to edit the site in H0–H1. They are evidence-dependent surfaces to revisit only after P0/P1 evidence and later Humanizer gates.
