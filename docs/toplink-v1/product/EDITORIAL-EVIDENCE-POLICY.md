# Editorial and Evidence Policy

## Purpose

This policy gates knowledge, news, operational updates and customer stories. It does not authorize
claims, people, media or production copy by itself.

## Evidence and editorial axes

- Fact status: `APPROVED`, `PENDING`, `REJECTED`, `REFERENCE_ONLY`.
- Editorial lifecycle: `draft`, `in_review`, `approved`, `published`.
- A record publishes only at `published`, and each production-facing fact/media item must separately
  be `APPROVED`.
- Editing quality cannot promote an unverified fact to approved truth.

## Health-facing publication gate

Before publication, the editor verifies:

- supportive language is not presented as cure/treatment certainty;
- no absolute result or guaranteed timeline;
- product/device facts have authoritative documentation;
- limitations, cautions and professional escalation appear when relevant;
- sources and uncertainty are preserved;
- wording stays inside confirmed legal/operational scope;
- a personal experience is not generalized into a universal claim.

## Customer-story policy

A `customer_story` can publish only when all are documented:

1. the person/story is real;
2. informed consent/usage permission covers the intended publication;
3. relevant context and limitations are preserved;
4. edits do not create a universal health claim;
5. identifiable photo/video/audio is separately authorized;
6. the media provenance and identity class pass the `Media` contract;
7. the story remains a typed editorial artifact with author/date/source context.

A testimonial/story never becomes hardcoded homepage proof. A contextual relation to a story is
allowed only after publication and must not imply typical or guaranteed outcomes.

## No-evidence behavior

- No real approved story → no testimonial/customer-story section.
- Pending consent → story stays private/unpublished.
- Pending identity/media authorization → omit media or hold the story if identity is essential.
- Reference-only anecdote → research/editorial input only, never Toplink proof.
- Rejected claim/story → exclude from fixtures, CMS public output, metadata and schema.

## Stock and generic media

Stock may illustrate generic wellness atmosphere only when licensed and labeled by provenance. It may
not masquerade as Toplink staff, customer, premises, procedure or outcome evidence. Captions and alt
text must not manufacture an identity the asset does not have.

## Roles and workflow

- Author: creates/edits own drafts; cannot publish.
- Editor: reviews facts, claims, consent and media, then approves/publishes.
- Administrator: configuration/system role, not routine editorial authority.
- Business/Media owners supply or approve facts/rights; the Editor cannot substitute for missing source
  authority.
