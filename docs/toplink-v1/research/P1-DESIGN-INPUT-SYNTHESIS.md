# P1 Design-Input Synthesis

## Boundary

> **REFERENCE GRAMMAR ≠ TOPLINK DESIGN**

Sen supplies observational evidence about morphology, UI/UX behavior, responsive transformation and
motion. Toplink brand truth supplies identity, tone, safety, audience and material constraints. P1
does not combine them into a production art direction.

## Ten findings that matter downstream

1. **Page job should determine morphology.** Sen's landing, branch directory, editorial index,
   article and form surfaces are structurally different; this is more useful than any shared styling.
2. **Progressive disclosure is the strongest directly adoptable behavior.** Native nested branch
   details stay understandable at all four widths (SEN-EV-025/028/031/034).
3. **Split compositions need authored mobile order.** Successful responsive transfer is a meaning
   sequence, not merely `grid → block` (SEN-EV-038/042).
4. **Knowledge discovery deserves its own morphology.** Main feed, recent discovery and pagination
   support Toplink's national knowledge audience, but Sen's promotion-heavy visual treatment does not.
5. **Conversion should stay subordinate to understanding.** Sen keeps forms nearby, but Toplink must
   replace them with verified external handoff and no stored lead state.
6. **A sticky orientation layer can help but the observed 1.25s entrance is too prominent.** Toplink
   should test a quieter state change and complete reduced-motion suppression.
7. **The live source is not a reliable motion implementation authority.** Mixed-content chunk
   failures broke mobile nav and carousel states; only measured healthy behaviors enter the corpus.
8. **Toplink identity must carry productive tensions.** Warm/not casual, expert/not superior,
   premium/not distant and Eastern/not mystical are stronger constraints than a color palette.
9. **Tân Trung Hoa should be expressed as spatial/behavioral grammar.** Axial balance, thresholds,
   joinery and density control are hypotheses; motifs and dossier hex values remain unapproved.
10. **Trust is procedural.** Consent, limits, evidence classes and calm explanation must appear near
    decisions; ornament cannot substitute for them.

## Sen UX patterns that conflict with Toplink identity/scope

- On-site forms conflict with external-only contact transport and privacy minimization.
- Public price/menu pages conflict with the no-public-pricing decision.
- Promotion-dominated news art conflicts with calm, knowledge-first brand maturity.
- Full address walls and tiny operational text conflict with older/mobile audience readability.
- Persistent gallery transforms and the prominent header entrance overstate motion relative to the
  Toplink calm/consent posture.
- Sen green/gold/lotus assets and its people/place imagery are source-brand evidence only.

## Structural primitives requiring total visual re-authoring

- Single-promise hero.
- Alternating service-family compositions.
- Image + branch directory pairing.
- Main editorial feed + recent discovery.
- Contextual human-handoff surface.
- Multi-cluster operational footer.

Their content hierarchy may inform P2/P3, but all typography, color, media, ornament, copy, data and
motion must originate in the Toplink system.

## Toplink-only inputs

- Public name and descriptor hierarchy.
- Caregiver/Sage/Guide interaction posture.
- Tĩnh → Thông → Dưỡng → Tỉnh pacing hypothesis.
- Lý/Dược/Dưỡng meaning system.
- Health-claim classes, privacy, consent and professional limits.
- Tân Trung Hoa contemporary material/geometry direction.
- Five-audience accessibility and trust constraints.

## What P2 may carry forward

- The seven observed page-job/morphology families as comparative input, not a sitemap.
- Need-family discovery before service selection.
- Knowledge feed/recent/pagination roles.
- Progressive disclosure for large operational sets.
- External contact availability near high-intent moments.
- Audience constraints and trust/safety requirements.

## What P2 must not carry forward

- Sen route names, section order, service taxonomy, copy, media, pricing, forms, claims or metadata.
- Any of the three provisional visual-family hypotheses as an already selected direction.
- Dossier proposals for accounts, internal booking/lead systems, pricing or franchise routes.
- Final palette, font, pattern, token, motion system or Toplink browser composition.

## P1 verdict

The corpus is sufficient to inform P2 product IA/content architecture and later P3 art-direction
comparison. It does not authorize either phase.
