# Audience Design Implications

Source: `sources/AUDIENCE-PROFILE.md`. This is a constraint map, not a page design.

| Segment                             | Readability / cognitive load                                               | Navigation / discovery                                                    | Motion comfort                                                    | Trust / privacy / health language                                            | Mobile / visual maturity                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Người bận rộn ở Hà Nội (28–55)      | Scan-friendly hierarchy, concise explanations, visible next step           | Discover by familiar need without self-diagnosis; contact in one/two taps | Brief state feedback; no delay theatre                            | No sales pressure; explain scope before recommendation                       | Strong mobile path, polished but not app-gimmicky                         |
| Người trung niên và lớn tuổi        | Comfortable body size/line height, few simultaneous choices, plain labels  | Stable global nav, clear backtracking, safety/limits easy to find         | Low amplitude, no persistent auto-motion; reduced motion complete | Calm suitability language, no certainty claims, visible professional limits  | Large targets, strong contrast, mature warmth without tiny luxury type    |

| Phụ nữ chăm sóc sức khỏe chủ động   | Refined hierarchy without euphemistic vagueness                            | Discreet service exploration and optional contact                         | Smooth but restrained; no intrusive reveal                        | Consent, dignity, image provenance and privacy must be visible behaviors     | Premium maturity comes from detail/control, not ornate decoration         |
| Người quan tâm dưỡng sinh toàn quốc | Long-form reading comfort, summaries and related knowledge                 | Knowledge is first-class; do not force location/contact path              | Reading should remain static and interruption-free                | Separate education, experience and verified claim classes                    | Responsive editorial system; useful even without visiting a facility      |

## Cross-audience constraints

- No urgency, fear-selling, aggressive popup, diagnosis quiz or forced account.
- Contact is near intent but never visually dominates education.
- Safety and limitations use calm plain language at the decision point.
- A visual-family candidate fails if it needs tiny text, low contrast or continuous motion to feel
  premium.
