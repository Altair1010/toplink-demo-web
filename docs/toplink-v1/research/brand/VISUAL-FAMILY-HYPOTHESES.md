# Provisional Toplink Visual-Family Hypotheses

These are three mechanically different P3 inputs. P1 selects **no winner**, font, color value,
pattern, token or browser comp.

## Family A — Tĩnh Mạch / Ceremonial Restraint

- **Semantic thesis:** care begins with a deliberate threshold and a stable place to pause.
- **Composition mechanism:** broad quiet fields, one axial anchor, selective asymmetry, few but
  substantial sections.
- **Tonal/color-role logic:** deep anchor fields, luminous reading surfaces, one restrained ritual
  accent; values remain open.
- **Material logic:** matte wood/mineral depth expressed through layering and edge treatment, not
  texture wallpaper.
- **Type character:** dignified editorial display paired with generous, high-legibility body text.
- **Ornament density:** very low; threshold/frame used only at major transitions.
- **Imagery:** cinematic wide crops of real space/material/hands with protected quiet zones.
- **Motion character:** slow state settling, short orientation feedback, no continuous ambience.
- **Digital mechanism:** responsive editorial grid and calm sticky orientation.
- **Audience risk:** may feel too distant or sparse to users seeking quick service recognition.
- **Anti-pattern risk:** luxury hotel minimalism with Chinese-flavored trim.

## Family B — Dược Liệu Học / Living Apothecary

- **Semantic thesis:** Toplink makes traditional care understandable through a living knowledge
  system.
- **Composition mechanism:** modular taxonomy, annotated material specimens, layered knowledge
  trails and progressive disclosure.
- **Tonal/color-role logic:** light working surfaces with deeper botanical/material anchors and
  functional semantic roles; values remain open.
- **Material logic:** translucent layers, paper/fiber cues, measured botanical detail, precise labels.
- **Type character:** readable humanist text with a scholarly display counterpoint.
- **Ornament density:** medium but informational; rules, seals or markers encode hierarchy.
- **Imagery:** macro material/hands/process images paired with explanation, not miracle-product shots.
- **Motion character:** reveal relationships, expand annotations, trace a choice; short and purposeful.
- **Digital mechanism:** strong filtering/disclosure grammar and evidence/status annotation.
- **Audience risk:** can become cognitively dense for older visitors if annotation dominates.
- **Anti-pattern risk:** apothecary catalog, museum label wall or botanical lifestyle cliché.

## Family C — Tân Cung Đương Đại / Structured Procession

- **Semantic thesis:** the four-state journey is experienced as a controlled procession through
  thresholds, courts and nested rooms.
- **Composition mechanism:** sequential spatial chapters, framed portals, alternating compression and
  release, stronger page morphology differences.
- **Tonal/color-role logic:** pronounced anchor/surface contrast with sparing ceremonial highlights;
  values remain open.
- **Material logic:** joinery, screens and layered depth abstracted into grid/section boundaries.
- **Type character:** more assertive display hierarchy balanced by plain functional reading text.
- **Ornament density:** medium, concentrated at thresholds rather than repeated around every card.
- **Imagery:** architectural, ritual and human-scale scenes with directional crops.
- **Motion character:** chapter transitions and gentle depth shifts; amplitude constrained.
- **Digital mechanism:** route-specific morphology and scroll-independent chapter navigation.
- **Audience risk:** stronger visual intensity can overwhelm privacy-sensitive or motion-sensitive users.
- **Anti-pattern risk:** theatrical palace set, red/gold excess or decorative pseudo-history.

## Comparison gate for P3

All families must later be tested on identical approved content at desktop/mobile for readability,
brand fit, audience comfort, commercial usefulness, accessibility and extensibility. No comparison
or selection is authorized in P1.
