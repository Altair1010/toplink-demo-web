# Project Charter

## Mission

Build **Y Viện Toplink** as a commercially useful, media-ready, editorially maintainable headless website that communicates a distinct contemporary Đông-y-cung-đình identity without health fear-selling or generic AI aesthetics.

## Primary jobs

1. Help a visitor recognize that Toplink may be relevant to their care/wellness needs.
2. Explain services clearly enough to reduce uncertainty without self-diagnosis or exaggerated claims.
3. Build trust through process, boundaries, real evidence and visual coherence.
4. Let the visitor contact a human through Zalo, Facebook/Messenger or phone when ready.
5. Let staff publish knowledge/news/customer stories through WordPress without touching code.
6. Give marketing a foundation for SEO, social distribution, campaign landing surfaces and analytics.

## Audience consequences

The five target segments require:

- mature, legible typography and high contrast;
- controlled motion rather than spectacle;
- clear safety/boundary language;
- low-pressure conversion;
- privacy-aware imagery/copy;
- mobile-first contact paths;
- enough editorial depth for people who may never visit the facility but want learnable dưỡng sinh content.

## Non-goals V1

- Internal booking engine or CRM lead capture
- Ecommerce/checkout
- Public prices
- Membership/account system
- Franchise pages
- Generic testimonial carousel
- User comments/newsletter
- Heavy 3D/WebGL
- Page-builder ownership of frontend layout

## Quality bar

A page passes only if it is strong across:

- brand specificity;
- art direction;
- content usefulness;
- commercial clarity;
- trust/evidence;
- service discovery;
- responsive behavior;
- accessibility;
- performance;
- motion quality;
- SEO readiness;
- CMS editorial usability;
- implementation integrity.

### Blind identity test

Remove the logo. If the site still looks interchangeable with a generic wellness/medical landing page, the art direction has not passed.
