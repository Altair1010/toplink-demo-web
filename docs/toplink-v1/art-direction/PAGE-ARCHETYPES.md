## Content relationship rules

- Knowledge may link to relevant approved services, but must not diagnose the reader.
- Product content does not create an ecommerce funnel.
- Customer testimonials/stories live as real published editorial content; no fake testimonial fixture section.
- Service pages omit price completely.
