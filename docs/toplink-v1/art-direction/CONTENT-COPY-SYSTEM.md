# Content & Copy System

## Voice

Use the uploaded brand dossier as the starting authority for voice:

- sincere;
- accessible;
- calm;
- knowledgeable;
- warm;
- transparent.

Avoid sermonizing, mystical health certainty, fear, inflated promises and generic marketing slop.

## Copy reconstruction

Do not “humanize” existing H7 sentences by synonym replacement. New copy follows:

`approved product fact → user situation → plain explanation → bounded support → limitation/safety where needed → optional human next step`

## Health-claim gate

Before publish, check:

- supportive language rather than cure claims;
- no absolute outcome/time guarantee;
- product/device facts have source evidence;
- no extrapolation from testimonials;
- caution groups and medical escalation guidance when relevant;
- wording stays inside the facility/product's confirmed legal scope.

## Price policy

- No price field is rendered in V1.
- Do not use “Liên hệ để biết giá”.
- Do not insert fake `0đ`, hidden price markup or schema offers.

## Testimonial/customer story policy

A testimonial may ship only when:

- it is real;
- consent/usage permission is documented;
- context is not edited into a universal health claim;
- video/photo identity is handled with permission;
- it is published as editorial content (e.g. customer-story category) rather than fabricated UI proof.

## Stock-image policy

Stock may depict generic wellness context but cannot be captioned or framed as:

- Toplink staff;
- Toplink customer;
- Toplink premises;
- evidence of treatment outcome.

## Editorial state

Each content item should maintain at least:

- `draft`
- `in_review`
- `approved`
- `published`

WordPress roles implement the human workflow; GitHub does not become the blog CMS.
