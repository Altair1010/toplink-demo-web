## Core thesis

Y Viện Toplink unfolds as a contemporary procession. A reader crosses a clear gateway, moves through
purpose-built courts and chambers, and reaches a release: more understanding, a bounded next reading
step, or optional verified human contact. Procession is an information mechanism, not palace theatre.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Chaptered procession:** every major shift changes the reader's question or task.
2. **Threshold clarity:** a threshold names what comes next; it never hides content behind spectacle.
3. **Calm authority:** clear explanation, evidence and limits create authority.
4. **Bounded ceremony:** ceremony is concentrated at entry and chapter transitions.
5. **Utility before ornament:** controls, cautions and long reading remain direct.
6. **Understanding before contact:** contact follows comprehension and trust.
7. **Readable dignity:** body text and interaction comfort outrank display drama.
8. **Consent and truth visibility:** missing facts and unauthorized media collapse cleanly.

## Structural mechanism

- **Gateway:** the route thesis and orientation. Maximum ceremony zone; one H1; no fake proof.
- **Court:** a broad comparison or discovery field with more than one valid path.
- **Chamber:** a focused explanation, process, evidence or limitation unit.
- **Compression:** a deliberate narrowing before a consequential idea or caution.
- **Release:** a calmer, wider reading or next-step surface. It must not imply conversion is mandatory.
- **Threshold:** a spatial/rule change that signals a new job, not merely a new background.

Pages are directed sequences, not interchangeable stacks of hero + cards + CTA.

## Page-world grammar

- Each route uses one dominant morphology from `PAGE-MORPHOLOGY-LIBRARY.md`.
- Adjacent chapters must differ in job, width, density or material posture.
- The four-state `Tĩnh → Thông → Dưỡng → Tỉnh` rail is orientation, never forced navigation.
- A utility route may use only a gateway-lite and one release; it must not imitate the homepage.
- Repeated grids are allowed only when records truly compare; they are never the default skeleton.

## Homepage principle

The homepage is the fullest procession: identity gateway → four orientation states → service-discovery
court → trust/limits threshold → knowledge reading hall → real-space evidence gate → optional contact
release. Each chapter pays for its presence with a different user job. Pending space/contact evidence
collapses rather than becoming decorative filler.

## Service detail principle

Use a bounded chamber sequence: purpose → who it may fit → verified experience/process → limitations
and professional escalation → related knowledge → optional contact. The limitation chamber is a first-
class reading surface. It cannot be visually demoted below a sales prompt.

## Knowledge detail principle

Use a reading hall: learning question → plain explanation → source/evidence state → bounded application
→ cautions/escalation → related knowledge. The reader may finish without contact. A related service is
contextual and optional, never the article's destination.

## Utility page principle

About, contact and operational/news surfaces use reduced ceremony, shorter gateways and stable reading
widths. Contact is a verified destination directory, not a lead form. Space is evidence-led and may be
held entirely when authentic media is absent.

## Hierarchy model

- **Display:** one route thesis; Alegreya 500; sentence case; maximum two display lines when practical.
- **Chapter heading:** names a change in user job; Alegreya 500/600.
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans 400; plain Vietnamese; long-form width capped at 44rem.
- **Supporting note:** IBM Plex Sans 500; never below 14px; no gold on a light surface.
- **Caution/limit:** normal body size, explicit heading, strong rule or dark reading field.
- **Evidence:** provenance/status is text first; decoration cannot substitute for a source.
- **Contact:** compact release surface after a justified intent; no urgency or dominance.

## Density model

- **Sparse:** gateway, major release, actual-authorized image chapter.
- **Balanced:** service discovery, knowledge index, about narrative.
- **Dense:** evidence ledger, references, operational facts — with stable line length and spacing.
- **Maximum ceremony:** homepage gateway and one signature threshold.
- **Maximum utility:** cautions, evidence, contact, metadata and long-form body.

No page may remain high-ceremony for its full length. Compression must be followed by readable release.

## Ornament grammar

Ornament encodes threshold, joinery and rhythm through rules, inset frames and aligned seams. Use at
most one ornament family per chapter and concentrate it at the gateway/threshold. Do not use literal
Chinese borders, generic lattice wallpaper, gold labels on ivory, repeated seals, rounded-card walls,
glass effects or motif decoration around health cautions. The authentic pattern slot remains
`CONDITIONAL` until provenance exists.

## Image system

- Images have one declared role: orientation, evidence, explanation or atmosphere.
- Actual Toplink premises/people/procedures require approved provenance and authorization.
- Generic stock may be atmospheric only and must not be framed as Toplink evidence.
- Abstract material planes may reserve composition without impersonating a real place.
- Long-form knowledge may be image-free when imagery adds no explanatory value.
- Crops favor directional planes, hands/material detail and human-scale context; avoid clinical-result framing.

## Contact presence

Contact is eligible only after a trust, limitation or resolved-intent surface and only when the exact
destination is `APPROVED`. Pending destinations render as honest non-actions or collapse. Knowledge
pages do not carry persistent/sticky contact. No pulsing, modal, countdown, scarcity or price prompt.

## Signature mechanisms

1. **Guided procession rail:** `Tĩnh → Thông → Dưỡng → Tỉnh` provides reversible orientation across
   four information states; it never diagnoses or forces a route.
2. **Threshold-to-chamber release:** a compressed gateway releases into a wider reading court once,
   helping the reader understand the change from identity to explanation.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Change morphology when the page job changes | Reuse the homepage silhouette on every route |
| Put limitations in a legible chamber | Hide caution in small muted text |
| Use wine/brass roles sparingly | Treat red + gold as the brand system |
| Let body text carry trust | Manufacture authority through ceremony |
| Collapse pending facts and media | Fill slots with placeholders or Sen/H7 assets |
| Use one meaningful release motion | Fade every section or hijack scroll |
| Keep touch targets at least 44px | Require hover or precision taps |
| Preserve a knowledge-only completion path | Turn articles into sales doorways |

## Conditional boundary

Logo geometry, authentic pattern, real place/material nuance and consented photography remain
conditional. Their insertion may tune asset treatments and identity accents only. It may not change
page jobs, hierarchy, morphology, spacing, type scale, contact policy or motion logic.
