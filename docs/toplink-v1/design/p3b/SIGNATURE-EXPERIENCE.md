# Direction C Signature Experience Lock

## Guided procession rail

- **User job:** understand where they are in the path from recognition to a safe next step without
  being diagnosed or forced into contact.
- **Structure:** four reversible anchors — Tĩnh, Thông, Dưỡng, Tỉnh — each opens a distinct information
  question. The sequence is explanatory, not a route sitemap or mandatory funnel.
- **Content dependency:** approved brand concepts plus page-specific plain-language explanations.
- **Responsive behavior:** four-column rail at wide viewports; two-column or linear touch-safe links at
  tablet/mobile; no horizontal scrolling.
- **Reduced motion:** direct anchor/state change with no animation dependency.
- **Failure mode:** decorative numbers, forced step completion, oversized stage labels or diagnosis cues.
- **Restraint:** use once on the homepage; other routes may echo chapter orientation but not clone the rail.

## Threshold-to-chamber release

- **User job:** recognize the transition from brand orientation to actionable explanation.
- **Structure:** one compressed gateway frame settles, followed by an immediate stable reading court.
- **Content dependency:** route thesis and the next chapter's real job; no media dependency.
- **Responsive behavior:** nested frame becomes top/bottom rules under 48rem; text never moves out of view.
- **Reduced motion:** final frame rendered immediately, native scrolling only.
- **Failure mode:** cinematic wipe, delayed content, parallax, repeated releases or palace-decoration drift.
- **Restraint:** maximum once per route and omitted on utility/contact pages.
