# Direction B — Dược Liệu Học / Living Apothecary

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — P3 COMPARISON ONLY.  
**Identity:** TEXT IDENTITY SCAFFOLD — NOT FINAL LOGO.

## Semantic thesis

Traditional care becomes trustworthy when meaning, evidence and limits form a living knowledge system.

## Product fit

The workbench makes approved service and knowledge relationships inspectable without diagnosing or selling.

## Audience fit

Caregivers and nationwide knowledge readers gain explicit source/limit cues; older visitors require
disciplined chunking and disclosure so annotation never becomes a wall.

## Structural principle

An index/workbench hybrid: a taxonomy rail, one active specimen and layered annotations. Service detail
is an evidence sheet; knowledge detail is an annotated field note. Home does not dictate their silhouettes.

## Spatial rhythm

Short working intervals, rules and tabs alternate with full-width breathing zones. Density is selective.

## Typography character

Candidate: **Be Vietnam Pro** for UI/body + **Literata** for scholarly annotations/display, both SIL
Open Font License via Google Fonts. Vietnamese diacritics are tested at 375 and 1440; no family is locked.

## Color-role hypothesis

Bone paper, charcoal-tea ink, desaturated botanical/mineral anchor and oxidized red for evidence/focus
roles. This intentionally avoids copying Sen green/gold identity.

## Material model

Fiber paper, inked rules, translucent notation layers and precise specimen labels—not product glamour.

## Ornament grammar

Informational marks only: fine rules, registration dots and seal-like status shapes that encode hierarchy.

## Image direction

Small macro crops of generic material or hands paired with provenance labels. No miracle product shot or facility claim.

## Motion character

Focus motion connects an active lens to its explanation; native disclosures reveal annotations on intent.

## Responsive behavior

The rail becomes a touch-safe selector; annotations follow parent content; optional notes collapse behind
native details without hiding limits.

## Signature idea

A relationship field connects Tĩnh/Thông/Dưỡng/Tỉnh to information obligations, not diagnoses or products.

## Risks

Too many labels can feel clinical, museum-like or cognitively heavy; seals can drift into invented authority.

## Anti-pattern failure mode

An apothecary catalog made of uniform cards, botanical cliché and ornamental pseudo-evidence.

## What this direction deliberately refuses

Mystical herb promises, decorative status badges, hidden limitations, dense grids without disclosure and commerce cues.
