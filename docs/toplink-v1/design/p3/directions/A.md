# Direction A — Tĩnh Mạch / Quiet Meridian

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — P3 COMPARISON ONLY.  
**Identity:** TEXT IDENTITY SCAFFOLD — NOT FINAL LOGO.

## Semantic thesis

Care begins at a deliberate threshold: the interface makes room for attention before asking for action.

## Product fit

The calm sequence supports understand → trust → explore. Contact stays a quiet optional end state and
knowledge can remain the destination.

## Audience fit

Large type, low density and stable reading order favor older visitors, caregivers and privacy-sensitive
readers. Busy visitors may need stronger discovery cues below the first field.

## Structural principle

Axial quiet fields with one off-axis statement, a vertical identity spine and sparse threshold frames.
Home uses editorial thresholds; service detail becomes a long document with caution marginalia;
knowledge detail is a calm manuscript. The home silhouette is not repeated.

## Spatial rhythm

Long pauses, broad negative space, few substantial chapters and one intentional interruption per field.

## Typography character

Candidate: **Noto Serif** display + **Be Vietnam Pro** body, both SIL Open Font License via Google
Fonts. The pairing is tested at 375 and 1440; it is not a final font decision.

## Color-role hypothesis

Deep walnut/plum anchor, warm mineral reading surface, brown-black text and one muted brass threshold
role. Candidate values live only in prototype CSS variables.

## Material model

Matte mineral and dark wood expressed through weight, broad fields and edge depth, not texture wallpaper.

## Ornament grammar

One abstract moon-gate curve or fine threshold line at brand/chapter entry; no framed cards or repeated lattice.

## Image direction

Wide, low-contrast material/hands/space crops with protected quiet zones. Until authorized assets exist,
the comp uses geometric material scaffolds never labeled as Toplink premises or people.

## Motion character

Threshold mask settles open once; orientation feedback is short. No continuous ambience or section fade-up.

## Responsive behavior

The vertical spine becomes a compact top identity line; negative space compresses before type size; DOM
order matches mobile reading order; no essential overlap survives at 375.

## Signature idea

The opening threshold turns “Tĩnh” into pacing: one composed reveal creates room, then the page is still.

## Risks

Service discovery can arrive too late; broad quiet fields can become luxury-hotel minimalism.

## Anti-pattern failure mode

Cream + serif + brass trim becomes generic premium wellness, with Chinese flavor reduced to a curve.

## What this direction deliberately refuses

Dense card catalogs, ornate frames, forced conversion, continual motion and decorative proof.
