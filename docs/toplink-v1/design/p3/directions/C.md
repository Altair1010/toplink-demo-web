# Direction C — Tân Cung Đương Đại / Contemporary Procession

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — P3 COMPARISON ONLY.  
**Identity:** TEXT IDENTITY SCAFFOLD — NOT FINAL LOGO.

## Semantic thesis

Understanding unfolds as a contemporary procession: each threshold changes the question, not merely the scenery.

## Product fit

The sequence dramatizes discover → understand → trust → explore while preserving optional contact and a knowledge-only exit.

## Audience fit

Busy visitors receive strong orientation and memorable chapters; privacy- and motion-sensitive visitors
require bounded amplitude, clear reading surfaces and a complete reduced-motion state.

## Structural principle

A narrative procession through framed thresholds and released courts. Home is sequential; service detail
becomes a purpose/process/limits chamber sequence; knowledge detail becomes a reading hall that can end without contact.

## Spatial rhythm

Alternating compression and release, dark/light chapter states and changing content widths create spatial cadence.

## Typography character

Candidate: **Alegreya** display + **IBM Plex Sans** body, both SIL Open Font License. The display face is
used for rhythmic strokes, not to imitate calligraphy. Vietnamese diacritics are tested at 375 and 1440.

## Color-role hypothesis

Rice-paper reading field, brown-black joinery, deep wine/cinnabar anchor and sparse aged-metal highlight.
Saturation is contained to thresholds, never used as small gold text on light surfaces.

## Material model

Abstract joinery, screen depth and framed portals formed by layout boundaries rather than literal palace decoration.

## Ornament grammar

One joint/portal vocabulary concentrated at chapter transitions. No border around every card and no generic lattice field.

## Image direction

Directional architectural/material crops act as chapter planes. Generic scaffolds stay unlabeled and cannot
represent Toplink premises, staff, customers or treatment results.

## Motion character

Release motion expands the first compressed gateway into a readable court once; later motion is state feedback only.

## Responsive behavior

The procession becomes vertical; portals flatten into top/bottom rules; stage navigation stays visible as
text without horizontal scroll or scroll hijacking.

## Signature idea

Tĩnh → Thông → Dưỡng → Tỉnh becomes a reversible orientation rail through four information states, not a forced sitemap.

## Risks

The world can become theatrical, over-ornamented or tiring; sequential framing can overstate a path that is not mandatory.

## Anti-pattern failure mode

Red/gold palace décor, fake historical motifs, chapter numbers without information value and showy scroll choreography.

## What this direction deliberately refuses

Literal imperial costume, cinematic scroll hijacking, pulsing contact, fake space imagery and identical page morphology.
