## Canonical home obligations

The shared prototype data contains these modules in this semantic order:

1. **Orientation** — name, descriptor, outcome phrase and a direct explanation of the website job.
2. **Need recognition** — four brand-native lenses: Tĩnh, Thông, Dưỡng, Tỉnh. They are a pacing aid,
   not a diagnosis flow, route graph or service taxonomy.
3. **Service discovery** — neutral heading “Nhóm dịch vụ”; no service name, suitability claim, price,
   duration or result is invented. Publishable groups require approved facts.
4. **Trust and boundaries** — “Giải thích rõ ràng”, “Giới hạn và lưu ý”, “Quyền riêng tư và đồng thuận”.
5. **Knowledge discovery** — “Kiến thức dưỡng sinh”, “Giới hạn và lưu ý”, “Khi nào cần đánh giá chuyên môn”.
6. **Space/evidence boundary** — actual Toplink space may appear only with authorized current evidence.
7. **Optional handoff** — Zalo, Facebook/Messenger and phone are named as future verified channels;
   no active destination, number, fake link or response promise appears.
