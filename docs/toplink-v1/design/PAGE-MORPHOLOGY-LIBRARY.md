**Status:** LOCKED FOR P4. Product routes/jobs remain owned by P2.

## Shared grammar

Every route combines a gateway, court, chamber and release according to its job. These are semantic
morphologies, not reusable visual sections. Adjacent surfaces should not repeat the same width,
alignment and density more than twice. Evidence and cautions interrupt ceremony when truth requires it.

## Allowed variation

- Gateway depth: full only on home/about; lite on indexes/details; utility on contact.
- Court width: wide for discovery, reading-width for explanation, evidence-width for records.
- Dark chapters: maximum two consecutive; caution may use dark or emphasized light treatment.
- Asymmetric indent: service/process chapters only at desktop; never long-form body.
- Image chapters: only with an authorized role and provenance.

## Forbidden repetition

- One hero/chapter/card silhouette across all routes.
- Three-card grids as the default response to every content group.
- Repeating dark/light bands without a user-job change.
- Decorative chapter numbers where order is not meaningful.
- Homepage-scale ceremony on contact, legal/utility or long-form reading pages.
- A contact strip after every chamber.

## Trust, evidence and contact insertion

- Service detail: limitations and professional escalation precede contact.
- Product detail: documentation/evidence state precedes any contextual support.
- Knowledge detail: evidence and bounded application are part of the reading hall; contact is optional.
- Customer story: consent/provenance is a publication gate, not a decorative badge.
- Space: authentic evidence is the page job; without it the route is held or safely bounded.

## Anti-template test for P4

Home, service detail and knowledge detail must remain recognizable with backgrounds removed: home by
its staged procession, service detail by its purpose/process/limits chambers, and knowledge detail by
its stable reading hall and evidence spine. If they collapse to the same section stack, implementation
has drifted.
